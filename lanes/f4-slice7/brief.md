## The work, red then green for each step (keep `lanes/f4-slice7/redgreen.md`)

A. Vectors from the reference (`tests/ref/vectors/f4-slice7/`, generated by a script in your lane directory, at
   most 400 KB): dump texts of 40 `ffun` values (`(D, M)` in `(1,1), (2,3), (3,2), (6,6), (12,5), (1,1024)`;
   exact, ball, complex, 2000-bit values, radii of every size) and 40 `rfun` values (0 to 4 terms, degrees 0 to
   6, complex parameters with radii, the zero polynomial `P=[]`), with their canonical dump bytes from
   `proto/text_grammar.py`; the malformed texts: a letter in a count, a count of values different from `D M`,
   a term with an exact trailing zero coefficient, `Re(A) <= 0`, `D = 0`, truncated after a count, a count
   above the caps, a context occurrence (there is none in the grammar: the status), binds supplied.
