# Lane ux-bench: the first buildable slice of brief 4, the Bench

Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `lanes/COMMON.md`, `lanes/UX-COMMON.md`, `docs/ux/README.md`,
`docs/ux/brief-4-sweep-bench.md` (all; the slice is its last section), `docs/ux/mock-4-sweep-bench.html` (the
wall, wells, stop line, findings rail: reuse its markup and CSS), `tools/adf/README.md` (the commands `roots`,
`realroots`, `project`, `exp_at`, `log_at`, `powrat_at`, their forms and outputs, lines 144-272),
`docs/conventions.md` 9, `include/adelefeld/status.h`.

You own: `ux/bench/**` and `lanes/ux-bench/**`. Nothing else.

Build, in this order:

1. The sweep runner `ux/bench/sweep.py` (Python 3 stdlib): an experiment file `ux/bench/experiments/<name>.json`
   names a question (prose), the inputs (value forms or polynomials, the columns), the places (`R` and a list
   of primes, or `primes below <bound>` expanded by the runner with a sieve), the operation (`roots n`,
   `realroots`, `exp`, `log`, `project`, `powrat e/n seed`) and the parameters (the precision per place; a
   budget: at most `max_cells` cells and `timeout` seconds per driver process). The runner writes the driver
   lines, runs `build/adf` (one process per experiment or per column; `timeout` as given, 2 cores), parses the
   result lines and writes `ux/bench/out/<name>.records.jsonl`: one record per well with the row (place), the
   column (input), the driver line, the output line verbatim, the status (`OK` or the status name), the
   status class (D-UX4), the count when the output is a list (roots, branches), the precision, and the time.
   Cells not run for budget are recorded as `below the stop line` with the reason. The stop line is the first
   place after the last place that was run.
2. The wall renderer `ux/bench/render.py` writing `ux/bench/out/<name>.html`: rows are places (`R` first, then
   primes increasing), then the stop line, below which nothing is drawn but the count of places not run; the
   columns are the inputs with their value form; each cell a well whose shape is the status class and whose
   label is the count or the value (shortened, full text on hover); the findings rail on the right, filled
   from `ux/bench/experiments/<name>.findings.json` (written by hand in the slice: each finding with a state
   `pattern`, `sourced`, `refuted`, `open`, the places it covers and its text; the shapes of `pattern` and
   `sourced` must differ, brief "Risks"); the team strip replaced by one line "sweeper: N calls, M done, K
   below the stop line" from the records.
3. Two experiments as fixtures: `squares.json` (Scenario A: for which primes below 100 is 2 a square; columns
   `2`, `-1`, `3`, `5`; rows `R` and the primes; operation `roots 2`; the findings: the pattern by the residue
   of p modulo 8 for 2, modulo 4 for -1, as `pattern` with the places swept, and one finding `sourced` only
   if you can cite a file under `refs/` by line for quadratic reciprocity, else `pattern`), and
   `local_global.json` (Scenario B: the column `(x^2 - 13)(x^2 - 17)(x^2 - 221)` with `roots 1`... read the
   scenario: the polynomial has a root at every place and no rational root; rows `R` and the primes below
   50; operation `roots` of the polynomial at each prime and `realroots`; the finding: a root at every swept
   place, no rational root, as `pattern` with its footprint, and the argument of the scenario as text).
   Regenerated by `ux/bench/run.sh`.
4. `ux/bench/README.md` (D-UX6) and `ux/bench/test_bench.py` (`python3 -m unittest`): the sieve; the parser
   of a roots line with a count, of an `error:` line, of a real-roots line; the budget cut-off placing the
   stop line; the renderer producing a page with every row and column of a three-by-three experiment.

Checks to report with numbers: the records count per experiment and the time of each sweep; both pages
regenerated and `diff` empty; the tests; what the page shows for Scenario A and B.

Out of the slice: the three agents (the pattern finder is the next step, not this one), parallel runs,
cost estimates. Record under D-UX3 what the driver could not give (the failing place; reason codes; places
as data).
