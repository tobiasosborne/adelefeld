# Lane ux-bench: result (Claude Opus, 2026-10-16)

The first buildable slice of brief 4, the Bench (`docs/ux/brief-4-sweep-bench.md`, last section, items 1 to 3; no
agents). It is built in `ux/bench/` in the order of `lanes/ux-bench/brief.md`, red-green: tests first, seen failing
on the missing module, then the code.

## What was built

1. **The sweep runner `ux/bench/sweep.py`** (Python stdlib).
   - Input: an experiment JSON naming a question, inputs (value forms, or polynomials for `roots`/`realroots`),
     places (`R`, primes, `primes below N` expanded by a sieve of Eratosthenes), an operation (`roots n`,
     `roots`, `realroots`, `exp`, `log`, `project`, `powrat e/n seed`), a precision per place (`R` bits, `p` the
     p-adic N, a prime as a key) and a budget (`max_cells`, `timeout`).
   - One driver process per experiment, run row by row: R first, then the primes increasing. The driver reads
     all of its input before it runs anything, so the runner writes every line and closes stdin. It then reads
     stdout made line-buffered by `stdbuf -oL`, each line as it arrives: that gives a time per cell, and a
     process killed at the deadline keeps the lines it already wrote. The process runs in its own session, and
     the whole group is killed at the deadline.
   - `max_cells` cuts by whole rows. A timeout puts the row being run and every later row below the stop line;
     answers that arrived for the cut row stay in its records, but nothing below the line is drawn.
   - Output: `out/<name>.records.jsonl`, one record per well (row, column, input, the driver line, `prec`, the
     output verbatim, status, D-UX4 class, count for lists, value for the other operations, reason for a cell
     not run), and one per extra check line.
   - The times, the process facts, the sha256 of `build/adf` and the commit go to `out/<name>.run.json`. With
     times in the records the records could not be reproduced byte for byte.
2. **The wall renderer `ux/bench/render.py`** writes `out/<name>.html`, one static page with inline CSS and JS
   and no network. The mock's Google Fonts link was dropped, because D-UX1 allows no network.
   - Rows are R and then the swept primes; then the stop line. Below it only one sentence: the next prime not
     swept, the count of places not run with the reason, and the primes skipped in a gap of the list.
   - Columns carry the value form and a footprint ("a rational: exact at every place", the polynomial's
     coefficients, or "footprint not given").
   - Wells by the D-UX4 class:
     - OK with a count: filled disc;
     - OK with an empty list (`none`): outlined ring with 0;
     - OK with a value: pill with the value cut short;
     - proved no: ring with a slash;
     - open: dashed `?`;
     - LIMIT: dotted square;
     - not mathematics: `!`;
     - no command at this place: a dash.

     The full record is in the hover text. A legend shows all seven.
   - A regroup selector by `p mod m`, also reachable by the address hash `#mod8`.
   - Hatched bands below the stop line for findings with an argument or a source.
   - A checks list, and the findings rail. The line "sweeper: N calls, M done, K below the stop line" replaces
     the team strip.
   - Each finding has a state of four, each with its own shape: `pattern` rounded outline, `sourced` double
     square border, `refuted` struck out, `open` dashed.
   - Besides its words, a finding can carry a machine rule (`places`, `when` by residue or by a list, `then`,
     `else`, `checks`), which the renderer checks against the records. The card then says "Rule checked by the
     renderer on N places: no exception", or gives a red warning that names the places that contradict it.
   - The renderer refuses a `sourced` finding without a source, and a source file not on disk or without the
     cited lines. Hovering a finding lights the wells it rests on.
3. **Fixtures** (regenerated by `ux/bench/run.sh`; `run.sh --check` regenerates into a temporary directory and
   diffs).
   - `squares` (Scenario A): columns 2, -1, 3, 5; rows R and the 25 primes below 100; `roots 2`
     (`roots_at n with p with 2`; at R `realroots` of `x^2 - n`); prec 8 and 64. Four findings:
     - F1 and F2: `pattern`, by p mod 8 for 2 and by p mod 4 for -1.
     - F3 and F4: `sourced`. They cite `refs/src/shoup-ntb/ntb-v2.txt:15032-15034` (Theorem 12.1 (iv), recast:
       2 is a QR mod p iff p = +-1 mod 8) and `:15027-15028` (-1 iff p = 1 mod 4), and
       `refs/src/conrad-hensel/hensel.txt:160-170` (Example 2.9: for odd p a unit is a square in Q_p iff it is
       a square mod p).
   - `local_global` (Scenario B): the product column, written constant first as `-48841 0 6851 0 -251 0 1`
     (a test checks that it is the product of the factors), and the three factor columns. Rows R and the 15
     primes below 50; `roots` at a prime (K = 8) and `realroots` at R. Three checks: `root 13|17|221 with 2`.
     One `pattern` finding, with a rule over the 16 places and the 3 checks; its argument is the scenario's,
     citing Shoup 15014 (Theorem 12.1 (ii)), Conrad 160-170 and `docs/SPEC.md:541-542`.
   - `exp_edge` (a third fixture, beyond the brief): five inputs including `(* ; 0 mod 5)`, `exp`, R and the
     primes below 30, `max_cells` 40. It shows a budget stop line, value wells and NOT_DETERMINED.
4. **`ux/bench/README.md`** (74 lines, D-UX6) and **`ux/bench/test_bench.py`** (30 tests).
   - The tests cover the sieve (against trial division to 2000), the places, and the class table against every
     name of `status.h`.
   - The parsers: a roots_at line with a count, a `roots` line, `none`, `error:` lines, a real-roots line of
     6 balls, value lines, and an unknown status refused.
   - The commands, the budget cut, the stop line, and a timeout with a fake hanging driver.
   - The real driver on a three-by-three experiment, and the renderer on a three-by-three experiment (every row
     and column, the stop line, the sweeper line).
   - Rule checks, the distinct state shapes, the source checks, and the committed fixtures (findings hold,
     pages equal a fresh render, the Scenario A and B walls).

## Library demands met by the slice (D-UX3, against brief 4, "What the library must provide")

1. Batch interface with records: **partly**. The slice makes the records itself by parsing the driver's lines.
   - Not given: the failing place ("the place that the library reports is not printed",
     `tools/adf/README.md:253`).
   - Not given: the value form of a result. The commands at places print a driver text, not the value form
     (`:263-264`).
   - Not given: a dump, and the precision of a result as data (only the requested `prec` and the printed `O()`).
   - Not given: a time per call. The driver reads its whole input before it runs and flushes only at exit
     (`tools/adf/adf.c`: fread to the end, fflush at the end). The slice needs `stdbuf -oL` to get line-wise
     output, and a timeout ends the whole experiment.
2. Thread safety: not exercised (no parallel runs; out of the slice).
3. Cost estimates and budgets: **unmet**. The runner has only `max_cells` and a wall-clock timeout; nothing
   prices `roots_at 2 with 7 with 2` at prec 20000 (7.4 s) before it runs. `LIMIT` from `ADF_ROOTS_BITS_MAX`
   exists for `roots` only.
4. The Tier A catalogue (Legendre, Jacobi, Kronecker, Hilbert): **unmet**. The squares wall uses `roots_at` as a
   stand-in for the Legendre symbol.
5. Places as data: **unmet**. The runner sieves in Python and checks the primality of listed places itself.
6. Stable statuses and reasons: the statuses are met (11 names, all in the class table, checked against
   `status.h`). The reasons are **unmet**. DOMAIN for 2 at 2 (odd valuation) and DOMAIN for -1 at 3 (a
   non-residue) are the same word, so a pattern finder could not tell the causes apart.

## Findings against the brief

1. **"No root" has two forms in the driver.** `roots_at` gives `error: DOMAIN` when the existence criterion
   fails. `roots` (polynomial) and `realroots` give `none`, a complete empty list with status OK. So in Scenario A
   the R cell of -1 is OK with 0 roots, not the proved-no ring that the mock draws, and the factor columns of
   Scenario B are OK with 0. The slice keeps the class OK and draws an empty list as an outlined ring with 0, so
   that a reader is not misled by a filled disc.
2. **`roots n` at R has no driver command for a non-rational input.** `roots_at` at real is UNSUPPORTED
   (`tools/adf/README.md:511`), so the runner uses `realroots` of `x^n - q` for a rational `q`. A ball input has
   no R cell: it is drawn as a dash with the reason.
3. **The time in the record and reproduction conflict.** UX-COMMON asks for a regenerated file with an empty
   diff, and the brief puts the time in the record. The slice keeps the times in `<name>.run.json`.
4. **One process per experiment, not per column.** Since the driver reads to the end of its input before it
   runs, a timeout in a process per column would lose every line of that column without `stdbuf`, and the stop
   line would not be one line across the wall. One process, row by row, gives one stop line.
5. **The budget cut is by whole rows.** A cut inside a row would put the stop line inside a row.
6. **A hand-written finding could be false.** The slice gives a finding a machine rule that the renderer checks
   against the records. It is a small skeptic without an agent, and it is what keeps "pattern" honest. The lane
   brief has four states; brief 4 also has `argued`. Scenario B's finding has an argument and stays `pattern`, as
   the lane brief asks; its band says "argued, not computed". Its two argument steps are on disk (Shoup 12.1
   (ii), Conrad 2.9), so it could be `sourced` with R, 2, 13 and 17 covered by computation.
7. **The footprint of a column** is given only for rationals and polynomials. For a ball it would need the
   places of the value from the driver; factoring the modulus in Python would put a number on the screen that
   the driver did not compute (D-UX5).
8. **The cost note of brief 4 does not reproduce.** It cites "59 s for `log(1 + p)` at N = 10000", but
   `log_at 6 with 5` at prec 50000 takes 0.04 s with this build. The slow cell found is
   `roots_at 2 with 7 with 2` at prec 20000 (7.4 s); `exp_at 5 with 5` at prec 100000 takes 2.2 s.
9. **The mock loads Google Fonts.** D-UX1 forbids network, so the page uses local font stacks.
