How to read

A value is a strip: the real place R, one column per prime below 1000 that divides its modulus or denominator (valuation v, |x|p, the p-adic digits with the low digit on the right, the period overlined), and one column for every other prime.

A fan shows every branch of a root; a click on a chip writes its seed into the cell. A click on the fog of a single root (above 97: on 12 more digits) asks for 12 more digits. Both edit the cell text only. Hover a strip or a chip for its set in words.

Statuses

open: more work may help: NOT_DETERMINED UNIT_NOT_CERTIFIED NEEDS_SPLIT NOT_UNIQUEproved: the answer is no: NO_SOLUTION NOT_UNIT DOMAINbudget: a limit was hit: LIMITnot a question of mathematics: UNSUPPORTED PARSEOK: a value, a fan or a strip; no cardfog: digits not known, or no information at a place

Agent window

extras.records.jsonl: one record per output, with the value text, the status, the dump (or why there is none), what computed it, and the driver line that reproduces it (or why there is none).
