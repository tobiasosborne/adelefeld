# Lane ux-atlas: the first buildable slice of brief 2, the Place Atlas

Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `lanes/COMMON.md`, `lanes/UX-COMMON.md`, `docs/ux/README.md`,
`docs/ux/brief-2-place-atlas.md` (all; the slice is its last section), `docs/ux/mock-2-place-atlas.html` (the
look; reuse its SVG and CSS), `tools/adf/README.md` (the commands `project`, `roots`, `exp_at`, `log_at`,
`realroots`, their forms and outputs, lines 144-272; the value forms of local and partial balls at 240-242),
`docs/conventions.md` 9, `include/adelefeld/status.h`.

You own: `ux/atlas/**` and `lanes/ux-atlas/**`. Nothing else.

Build, in this order:

1. The scene builder `ux/atlas/scene.py` (Python 3 stdlib): input a value (a rational or a finite ball in the
   driver's value form), a lens (`identity`, `roots n`, `exp`, `log`), a list of primes (at most four) and a
   depth range `d_min..d_max` (at most 5 at the top); it runs `build/adf` (one process, many lines; `timeout
   120`) for `project` of the value over the places, for `roots` of `X^n - x` at each prime and each depth
   (the depth is the precision `N` of the driver's `roots` command), for `exp_at` and `log_at` at each prime
   and depth, and for `realroots` on the real line; it parses the driver's lines (the value forms of local
   balls `a + O(p^N)`, the branch lists, `error: STATUS`) and writes ONE JSON scene file: the places, for each
   prime and each depth the lit paths (the base-p digit strings of the balls, from the centre of
   `a + O(p^N)`, computed in the builder from the driver's text, never from memory), the statuses at the place
   (as gates), the real ruler (the real ball's dyadic end points as the driver prints them, with the printed
   digits), the thread when the value is an exact rational (its path at every drawn prime and its point on
   the ruler), the fog count (`infinitely many` for an exact rational; for a ball `every other p` with the
   count of primes below 1000 dividing the modulus not drawn), and a provenance block (the driver lines run
   and their output lines, verbatim, so that the test driver can check every number).
2. The viewer `ux/atlas/atlas.html`: one page, inline CSS and JavaScript, no library, that loads a scene file
   (a file picker, plus `?scene=scenes/x.json` when served; from `file://` the picker is the way, say so in
   the README) and draws: the ruler on top with the real ball as a bracket and the thread's point; below it
   up to four p-adic trees, the root at the top, `p` children per node, drawn only along the lit paths and
   their siblings, a shaded cone for the rest (brief, "Risks"), above depth 6 paths only; the fog bank label
   with its count; threads for exact rationals through the ruler and the trees; gates at places with a
   status, in the shape of the status class (D-UX4) and the status name; a depth handle per tree that re-reads
   the precomputed depths of the scene (no computation in the page). A hover on a node shows the ball
   `a + p^k Z_p` in the driver's value form. Keep the page under 1200 lines.
3. Two scenes as files under `ux/atlas/scenes/`: `sqrt9.json` (the square roots of 9: lens `roots 2`, primes
   2, 3, 5, 7, depths 1..5; Scenario A) and `idempotents30.json` (the idempotents modulo 30, `x^2 = x`: lens
   `roots 2` on `X^2 - X`, primes 2, 3, 5, depths 1..5, with the eight solutions modulo 30 explained in the
   README by the lit paths at 2, 3 and 5; Scenario B), regenerated by `ux/atlas/build_scenes.sh`.
4. `ux/atlas/README.md` (D-UX6); tests: `ux/atlas/test_scene.py` (`python3 -m unittest`: the digit paths of
   `9 mod 5^3`, `-1 mod 5^3`, the parser on a branch list and on an `error:` line, the fog count), and
   `ux/atlas/test_atlas.mjs` (`node --test`: the pure functions of the page, exported from a shared
   `ux/atlas/atlas.js` that the page includes, such as the layout of a path's nodes and the choice of
   siblings drawn; test them without a DOM).

Checks to report with numbers: both scenes regenerated and `diff` empty; the counts of lit paths per prime
and depth in each scene; the tests; a screenshot of each scene if Firefox allows (UX-COMMON); what the page
shows at each step of Scenario A and B.

Out of the slice: the agent's tour, scene editing, the braid, the product-formula balance, zoom beyond the
depth handle, more than four trees. If the driver cannot give a scene element (the failing place, the exact
dyadic end points), the scene carries `"not given"` and the page draws a plain marker; list these in the
report (D-UX3).
