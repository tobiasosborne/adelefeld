spectral poles, and the local Gamma poles are NOT reported globally (the trivial zeros of `L` cancel them:
never evaluate `Gamma(z) L(s, chi)` for the continuation). `adf_tate_completed` is `Lambda_chi = C^((s+e)/2)
I_chi`, computed by the balanced split DIRECTLY (not `I` times `C^z` without a width recheck). `OK` means
finite and every coordinate diameter at most `2^-bits`; failure preserves `z`. The forbidden path (design 4):
`acb_dirichlet` L, zeta, xi, Hurwitz, theta and AFE functions are not called by the continuation; the test
binary link-wraps them to abort (design 7: `-Wl,--wrap`; FLINT's finite character pairing and our Gauss sum
stay permitted; the comparison with FLINT's values goes in a SEPARATE test binary or is read from vectors).
